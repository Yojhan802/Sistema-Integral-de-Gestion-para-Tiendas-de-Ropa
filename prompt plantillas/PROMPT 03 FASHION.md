```text

+
```

- Discover a collection.
- Recreate an outfit.
- Explore a brand identity.
- Find something visually exciting.

# 07. FASHION DESIGN PHILOSOPHY

FASHION should feel:

- Editorial.
- Visual.
- Confident.
- Contemporary.
- Dynamic.
- Aspirational.
- Stylish.
- Commercial.
- Bold when appropriate.
- Clean where commerce requires clarity.

The visual language should communicate that the storefront belongs to a real fashion brand rather than a generic software template.

---

# 08. FASHION ≠ CHAOTIC

Do not interpret "fashion" as:

```text
random oversized text
+
random overlapping images
+
everything animated
```

Editorial composition still requires structure.

Every visual decision must reinforce:

- Hierarchy.
- Brand identity.
- Product discovery.
- Storytelling.
- Conversion.

Never sacrifice purchase usability for artistic experimentation.

---

# 09. FASHION ≠ LUXURY

FASHION can be:

```text
BOLD
YOUTHFUL
EDITORIAL
EXPRESSIVE
URBAN
MODERN
```

while LUXURY will focus more heavily on:

```text
PRESTIGE
EXCLUSIVITY
CEREMONY
REFINEMENT
```

Do not automatically use serif typography, gold or black just because the store sells fashion.

The tenant brand should determine the personality.

---

# 10. CUSTOMER PSYCHOLOGY

Fashion shopping often begins visually.

The interaction model should support:

```text

↓
STYLE
↓
PRODUCT
↓
VARIANT
↓
PURCHASE
```

Instead of presenting every product as an isolated database item, allow the store to feel like a collection.

# 27. HERO IMAGE TREATMENT

Support:

- Full-bleed.
- Responsive crop.
- Art direction when assets allow.
- Safe text areas.
- Proper focal point.

---

# 28. HERO VIDEO

Do NOT automatically add video.

If an existing backend/configuration already supports campaign video:

it may be used carefully.

Requirements:

- Muted.
- No unexpected sound.
- Optimized.
- Poster fallback.
- Reduced motion consideration.
- Mobile performance consideration.

Do not invent video infrastructure.

---

# 29. HERO SLIDER

When multiple real campaigns exist:

FASHION may use a visually sophisticated slider.

Prefer:

```text

image transition
text transition
```

over generic left-right banner sliding.

Controls should be restrained.

Autoplay must never be aggressive.

Allow manual navigation.

Pause when appropriate.

# 30. HERO MOTION
